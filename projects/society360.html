Society360

A ServiceNow society management portal developed as a team capstone. I led the project and contributed to building a more organised digital experience for residents and society administrators.

Project overview

What it does

Society360 brings common society-management activities into one portal, helping residents raise and track requests while making information and services easier to access.

  • Resident-facing society management portal
  • Society dashboard
  • Maintenance Module
  • Pay Dues Module
  • Knowledge-based AI support and request handling
